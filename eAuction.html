What is ProcDigi eAuction Platform?

ProcDigi eAuction is a digital sourcing tool that enables organizations to conduct transparent, real-time competitive bidding events with suppliers, ensuring optimal pricing, faster turnaround, and full audit compliance.

How eAuction Works

1

Create Auction

Define items, rules, and auction parameters.

2

Invite Suppliers

Approved vendors participate securely.

3

Live Bidding

Suppliers bid in real-time with extensions.

4

Award & Reports

Finalize results with full audit trail.

Key Business Benefits

Cost Savings

Drive 5–15% savings through competitive pricing.

Transparency

Fair, auditable, and rule-based bidding.

Speed

Reduce sourcing cycle time dramatically.

Governance

Full compliance and role-based control.

Platform Capabilities

Built to support complex sourcing events with speed, security, and complete control.

⚙ Auction Capabilities

  • Both reverse (purchase) & forward (sale) auctions
  • Pre-defined automatic time extensions
  • Multiple types to suit different competitive scenarios
  • Automatic bid control to avoid wrong bids

🔐 Security & Control

  • Role-based access control
  • Encrypted bidding environment
  • Comprehensive audit logs & reports
  • Controlled supplier access

Who Uses eAuction?

This digital tool is industry agnostic and all types of industries and commercial establishments use eAuction for procurement.
Strategic sourcing teams, indirect procurement, RM purchase, MRO sourcings, scrap sales, capex sourcing, and organizations focused on cost optimization and digitalization.
