Process of eApproval

ProcDigi eApproval ensures structured, controlled, and traceable approval workflows with full visibility and compliance.

The ProcDigi eApproval process digitally routes documents through predefined, role-based workflows, ensuring each request reaches the right approver at the right time. Approvals are completed electronically with real-time tracking, automated notifications, and a complete audit trail for full transparency and compliance.
