Transitioning from Tradition to Technology

The Heritage of Trust

Since ancient times, fairness in procurement has relied on the secrecy and integrity of "Sealed Bidding" to ensure confidentiality, equal opportunity, and the prevention of corruption.

The Traditional Method

Bids were physically submitted in sealed envelopes and opened only at an appointed time in front of witnesses to ensure transparency.

The Modern Challenge

The manual system is prone to early tampering, information leaks, missing records, human error, and evaluation delays.

The Digital Leap

eTendering solves these issues by providing enhanced security, transparency, and speed.

Multi-Key Bid Opening

Innovative Security

Bids remain fully encrypted until the submission deadline.

Digital Authority

Multiple authorities must provide digital approval simultaneously to decrypt and open bids.

Uncompromising Integrity

No single individual can access or manipulate bids, ensuring 100% fair play.
